#7 Tab Click Navigation BUG MEDIUM

  1. On the Settings page, click the Connectors or Notifications tab
  2. Observe that the page intermittently navigates away from Settings entirely
  3. Destinations observed: /admin/gift-cards, /admin/referrals
Notes: When clicking tabs via the Tabs component, the page intermittently navigates to unrelated routes (/admin/gift-cards or /admin/referrals) instead of staying on the settings page with the selected tab. Direct URL navigation (e.g. ?tab=connectors, ?tab=notifications) works correctly every time. The bug is intermittent and appears related to a race condition between the goto() call in handleTabChange and the store subscription callbacks on lines 51-66 of +page.svelte.

Expected: Clicking a tab should switch tab content and update URL to ?tab=connectors or ?tab=notifications

Actual: Page navigates away from Settings to Gift Cards or Referrals

Workaround: Direct URL navigation (/admin/settings?tab=notifications) works reliably

Root cause: The currentMerchantId.subscribe() callback (line 60) fires when the merchant store updates, calling loadSettingsData() which may interfere with the goto() call in handleTabChange(). The .subscribe() pattern is a known anti-pattern in Svelte 5 runes-based components.

Reproduction rate: Intermittent (~50% of tab clicks during testing)
