COD Verification

Configure how COD order deliveries are verified before issuing reward points

ℹ️
Why verify COD orders?
Unlike prepaid orders, COD orders can be returned/rejected at the door. Verifying delivery before issuing points prevents rewards leakage on undelivered orders.
Issue rewards for COD orders
When enabled, COD orders will earn reward points after delivery is verified
Hold points until verification
Points appear as "pending" in the customer's wallet until delivery is confirmed
Choose Verification Method
