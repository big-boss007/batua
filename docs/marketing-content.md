## The Problem

**You're spending ₹300 to acquire a customer who buys once and never returns.**

Indian D2C brands face a unique set of challenges that global loyalty tools simply weren't built for:

- **15-25% repeat rate** — the global benchmark is 35-40%. More than half your customers never come back.

Smile.io charges ₹17,000/mo. Rise.ai doesn't handle COD. Nector doesn't have gift cards or memberships.

## Wallet

**Every customer gets a wallet. The wallet is why they come back.**

The wallet is the foundation of Breeze. Every credit — cashback, referral reward, gift card balance, birthday bonus — lives here. One balance. One checkout experience.

**What makes it different:**

**COD-safe credits**
Other tools give instant credit on order placement. In India, 30% of COD orders get returned. Breeze holds COD credit until delivery is confirmed. Released automatically via Shiprocket/Delhivery webhook. Cancelled automatically on RTO. You don't touch anything.

**COD-to-prepaid conversion**
"Pay via UPI instead of COD, get ₹75 instant credit." Merchants see 8-12% of COD orders switch to prepaid — saving ₹150-300 per order in logistics costs.

**Displayed vs spendable balance**
Customers see their total balance (including pending COD credits) — builds trust. But they can only spend the confirmed amount. Two views of the same wallet. No confusion at checkout.

**Multiple credit types**
Earned cashback, gift card balance, referral reward, goodwill credit, membership benefit — each tracked separately. Different expiry rules, different redemption constraints, all in one wallet.

**WhatsApp-first notifications**
Every earn, redeem, COD release, and expiry triggers a WhatsApp message. 85% open rate. Template-based with variable injection. Works with Interakt, WATI, or AiSensy — swap vendors without changing anything.

## Gift Cards

**Diwali. Rakhi. Birthdays. New revenue you're leaving on the table.**

Gift cards are a revenue channel, not just a feature. Every gift card sold is money in your store before a product ships.

**How it works**
You issue a card (single or bulk). Customer receives a code: BRZE-Q9MN-ZMC8-8NN3. They enter it on your storefront. Balance added to their wallet. They shop.

**Bulk issuance for festive season**
Upload a CSV with 500 gift cards for your Diwali campaign. Breeze issues all of them with unique codes. Re-upload the same file? Zero duplicates — every card has an idempotency key.

**Bearer instrument model**
A gift card isn't tied to a customer until it's claimed. Priya buys a ₹500 card, sends the code to her friend. Friend claims it — balance moves from bearer wallet to their wallet. Atomic. Auditable.

**Partial redemption**
₹500 card. Customer spends ₹200. ₹300 remains on the card. They come back for the rest.

**Balance check**
Customer visits your storefront → enters the gift card code → sees remaining balance and expiry date. No support tickets.

**Stats at a glance**
Total issued, outstanding value, active, claimed, expired — in your dashboard.

## Referrals

**Your best customers are your cheapest acquisition channel.**

Acquiring a customer through ads: ₹300. Through a referral: ₹50 (the reward you pay). And referred customers convert at 3-5x the rate of ad traffic.

**Dual reward**
Priya refers a friend → Priya gets ₹50, friend gets ₹25. Both are independent ledger entries — fully auditable.

**Vanity codes**
PRIYA10. VIKRAM20. Customers share codes they're proud of. Not `ref_8f3a2b`. Codes they put in their Instagram bio.

**Creator/influencer mode**
For your brand ambassadors: vanity codes + tiered commissions. Track conversions per creator. Payout via Razorpay (coming soon).

**Anti-fraud (5 signals)**
Same IP as referrer? Flag. Same device? Flag. 10 referrals in one hour? Flag. Referee created 2 minutes ago? Flag. Self-referral? Blocked. You don't need to police it manually.

**Branded referral page**
Priya shares her link → friend lands on a page at your domain: "You've been invited! Get ₹25 on your first order with code PRIYA10." How it works in 3 steps. Big "Shop Now" button.

**Analytics**
Total codes, total referrals, total conversions, conversion rate, suspicious flags — all in your dashboard. See which influencer drives the most sales.
