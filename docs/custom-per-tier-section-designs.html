Custom per-tier section — 5 designs

Same data, five different UX philosophies for letting the merchant set a per-tier cap. Same scenario in all five: Sherwani Studio's 5 tiers, rule cap ₹450, three tiers customised (Gold ₹750, Platinum ₹1000, Diamond ₹1500).

Pros: lets merchants think in tier-relative terms ("Gold gets 2× the cap"); caps stay proportional if the rule cap changes. Cons: learning curve — merchants need to understand the × syntax; "1.67×" feels like an odd number; the toggle adds another decision point. Best for power users who already think in multiples.

My pick for v1: A (inline column) for simplicity and speed-to-ship. My pick for v2: layer C's per-tier preview on top of A by making the "Edit" link expand inline. D and E are interesting but probably not worth the engineering for an opt-in advanced feature.
