Auto-tier assignment

Tiers recompute automatically from rolling spend, lifetime spend, or order count — pick the criterion that fits your brand. No CSV uploads, no manual tagging, no operations work. Customers see their next tier and exactly how much spend stands between them and it.

Earn-rate multipliers you control

Bronze 1x. Silver 1.25x. Gold 1.5x. Platinum 2x — or whatever ladder fits your margins. Set your own tier names, thresholds, and multipliers. Add a fifth tier ("Diamond"), call them whatever you want.

Stacks with campaigns & membership

A Gold customer on a 3x Diwali campaign earns 4.5x. If they're also a member, the membership boost stacks on top. The effective rate is shown on the storefront before they buy — they see exactly why it's worth checking out today.

Birthday bonus

"Happy Birthday Priya! ₹100 has been added to your wallet." Automatic. One per customer per year. Set the amount once, never think about it again.

Milestone rewards

3rd order: ₹50 bonus. ₹5,000 lifetime spend: ₹150 bonus. 10th order: ₹500 bonus. Checked automatically after every purchase. One-time per milestone.

Streak rewards

"You've placed 3 orders in 30 days — here's ₹75!" Rewards consistency, not just big spenders. Resets every window — earn the streak again and again.

Profile completion

"Complete your profile for ₹30 in credits." Name, email, birthday — each field tracked. You get better customer data. They get free money.

Upgrade, extend, renew — self-serve

Members switch plans, extend by months, or renew early from their account. You don't field a single support email. The wallet, balance, and benefits update instantly.

Earn-boost stacks with everything

A 2.5x member earn-boost compounds on top of Gold-tier 1.5x and a 3x Diwali campaign — the customer sees the effective rate before checkout. They feel the value of the membership on every order, not just occasionally.

Auto-renewal reminders

WhatsApp nudges fire at 7 days, 1 day, and on expiry. One-tap renew. Members renewing close to expiry convert at 65%+ — higher than any other lifecycle moment.

Tied to your loyalty tiers

Members can be granted instant tier status — pay for Gold instead of grinding to it. The customer's effective multiplier is always the better of earned tier and membership tier. No worse off, often better.

Why it works

Members have already committed money to your brand. They will come back to get their money's worth. Typical member spends 3-4x more than non-members.

Update 3 NEW section — "Merchant tools" / "For your team." Positions Batua as a real admin platform, not just a customer mechanic. Suggested place: after Campaigns, before Memberships.

For your team

Built for the people
who run loyalty, not just the customers.

Loyalty fails when the merchant team can't operate it. Every screen, every button, every export — built for the human running the store.

Wallet policies — redemption you control

Per credit type: minimum redemption, step size, % of order cap, ₹ cap, stackable with discounts (or not), expiry days, excluded payment methods. The CFO sets the guardrails once; customers see clean rules at checkout.

Manual & bulk credit

Credit one customer for a service recovery — or upload a CSV and credit 500 customers for your Diwali apology. Every entry is logged, reasoned, and attributed to the team member who issued it.

Debit & force-expire

Mistakes happen. Claw back a wrongly-issued credit. Force-expire a stale balance. Every reversal lands in the same append-only ledger — auditable, never silent.

Edit every notification

The actual WhatsApp / SMS / email copy is yours to edit per event — tone, language, emoji. Your brand voice survives every message we send on your behalf. Hindi, Tamil, Gujarati — go.

Brand your points

Call them "Drapes Dollars," "Henna Hearts," or "Chai Coins." Set the icon, the name, and the conversion rate to rupees. Your vocabulary, everywhere customers see their balance.

Transactions ledger

Every movement on every wallet, filterable, exportable, reconcilable. Your finance team gets the truth in one screen — no spreadsheets, no Shopify exports stitched together.

Update 5 NEW section — "Built on." Credibility section for buyers' finance teams and integration leads. Suggested place: just before the Pricing section.

Under the hood

Built like a bank.
Priced like a SaaS.

Loyalty programs are real liability — points are a number on your balance sheet. The system that mints them had better be one you'd trust your auditor with.

Append-only double-entry ledger

Every credit, debit, COD release, and expiry is an immutable entry. Nothing is overwritten. Nothing is silently corrected. The ledger is the source of truth — your auditor will recognize the shape immediately.

Idempotency on every action

Re-process the same Shopify webhook 100 times. The customer earns the bonus once. Every operation that touches the wallet carries an idempotency key — duplicates are physically impossible.

Every event, every state

Shopify webhooks land in a state machine: Received → Processing → Processed or Failed. Nothing is silently dropped, nothing is silently retried. Operations sees the truth on a dashboard, not in customer complaints.

Full attribution on every change

Every credit attributes to a rule, a campaign, a manual issuance, or a system event — and the human who triggered it. Customer asks "where did this ₹150 come from?" — one query, one answer.

Eight foundational truths

The architectural commitments we never break: the ledger is append-only; idempotency is structural; value is not fungible across buckets; and five more. Engineered before the first feature was written. Inviolable.
