I.Understanding Breeze

The retention problem in Indian D2C

The average Indian Shopify D2C brand spends ₹250–500 to acquire a customer who never comes back. Repeat-purchase rates sit at 15–25% versus a global benchmark of 35–40%. More than half of every cohort is gone after the first transaction.

Three forces compound the problem:

85%

COD is structurally hostile to loyalty: you can't give instant credit on an order that may never deliver. Existing loyalty tools either ignore COD (Smile.io, Yotpo, LoyaltyLion) or treat it badly (instant credit, manual reversals when RTO happens). Brands using these tools simply turn loyalty off for COD orders — losing rewards on 60%+ of their volume.

Communication is the second crack. Email opens average 18% in India; WhatsApp opens average 85%. Loyalty tools that send email notifications send to a channel customers don't read.

The third is tool sprawl. A merchant who wants wallet + loyalty + gift cards + referrals stitches together Smile.io + Rise.ai + ReferralCandy + Flits, paying ~₹17,000/month combined, none India-aware, none talking to each other.

The all-in-one proposition

Breeze is one platform that covers the entire retention surface a D2C merchant needs, built India-first:

  • One wallet for every form of store value — cashback, refunds, gift card balance, referral rewards.
  • One ledger with append-only audit trail, the same shape as a bank's books.
  • One platform for six products that share data, rules, and customer context.
  • One India-first toolkit — COD-aware crediting, WhatsApp delivery, UPI top-up, festive-native campaigns.

The structural advantage is greenfield design. Incumbents bolted features together over 8–10 years; their data models reflect build order, not domain truth. Breeze is built in 2026 with the full domain known.

II.Wallet

Pillar 1
Store credit and cashback that brings customers back.

The Batua wallet is the heart of the platform. Every credit a customer earns from any source — cashback, refunds, gift cards, referral rewards, birthday bonuses, goodwill — lives in one balance the customer spends at Shopify checkout.

Multi-bucket architecture

A single customer wallet contains multiple buckets. Each bucket represents a category of credit with its own rules:

BucketSourceTypical rules
EarnedCreditCashback from purchases, bonuses, streaks, milestonesExpires after 90–365 days; spendable up to % of order
CodPendingCashback on COD orders, held until deliveryReleased or cancelled by logistics webhook
GiftCardGift card balance after claimOften no expiry; full or partial redemption
ReferralRewardRewards earned for referring customersSeparate expiry; restricted to certain orders
GoodwillCreditManual credits from customer serviceReason attribution; per-actor audit trail
MembershipBenefitBonuses tied to a paid membershipLinked to membership state
CustomerFundedCustomer's own top-up via UPINo expiry; like a prepaid balance
RefundCreditRefunds returned as store creditSimilar to Rise.ai's signature flow

Each bucket type has independently configurable redemption policies set at the merchant level — minimum redemption, max per order, expiry, whether it stacks with discounts, and excluded payment methods. The customer sees one balance; the merchant has fine-grained control underneath.

COD-safe credits

This is Breeze's signature India-feature. Other tools give instant credit on order placement. In India, 25–40% of COD orders are returned. Breeze holds COD-earned credit until delivery is confirmed:

  1. Customer places a COD order. Breeze creates a Held ledger entry in the CodPending bucket.
  2. The customer sees the pending credit on their wallet, but it's not yet spendable.
  3. When the logistics provider's webhook fires order.delivered, Breeze atomically moves the credit out of CodPending and into EarnedCredit — fully spendable, customer notified by WhatsApp.
  4. If the order is returned (RTO), the held credit is voided. No manual reversal, no awkward customer support conversation.
Why it matters Merchants using competitor tools either skip COD orders entirely (losing rewards on 60%+ of volume) or pay out fraudulent / returned credit and absorb the loss. Breeze gives them the rewards mechanic on COD without the financial risk.

COD-to-prepaid conversion

Wallet cashback creates an incentive Indian merchants have wanted for years: "Pay via UPI instead of COD, get ₹75 instant credit." Typical results: 8–12% of COD orders switch to prepaid — saving ₹150–300 per order in logistics costs (RTO, COD handling fees, address verification, cash collection). Unlike one-time checkout discounts (GoKwik, Razorpay Magic), wallet cashback creates long-term stickiness — the customer comes back to spend that credit.

Displayed vs spendable balance

Customers see two numbers on their storefront wallet:

  • Spendable — what they can apply at checkout right now. Sum of all non-held buckets.
  • Pending — what's held against COD orders, labelled with how many orders are awaiting delivery.

Showing both builds trust without enabling spend the merchant hasn't earned yet.

WhatsApp-first notifications

Every wallet movement that matters to the customer triggers a WhatsApp message: earn, release, redeem, expiry warning, RTO cancellation. WhatsApp's 85% open rate makes it the only channel where rewards messages get noticed. Breeze integrates with Indian WhatsApp Business API providers (Interakt, WATI) via a connector framework — swap vendors without changing templates.

UPI top-up

Customers can fund their own wallet via UPI for a discount — "Add ₹500, get ₹525 credit." Useful for prepaid programs and pre-festive sale runs. Goes into the CustomerFunded bucket, never expires.

III.Loyalty

Pillar 2
Points, tiers, and the engagement mechanics that turn buyers into regulars.

Breeze's loyalty engine is a configurable rule system, not a fixed program. Merchants compose the program from primitives: earn rules, VIP tiers, milestones, streaks, scheduled bonuses, and gamification events.

Earn rules

The most fundamental primitive. An earn rule says: "when [event] happens with [conditions], award [reward]". Examples:

  • "5% cashback on every prepaid order ≥ ₹500, max ₹200 per order, expires in 90 days"
  • "₹100 bonus on the customer's first order"
  • "3% cashback on COD orders, released after delivery"

Rules are configured visually in the admin. Conditions can match payment method, order amount, collections, customer tags, and first-order status. Calculations support percentage of order or fixed amount. Each rule has its own cap (max per order) and expiry.

VIP tiers

Customers climb tiers based on spend (rolling, lifetime, or order count). Each tier carries an earn-rate multiplier applied to all subsequent rewards.

Example tierThresholdMultiplier
Bronze₹0+1×
Silver₹2,000+ rolling 365-day spend1.25×
Gold₹5,000+1.5×
Platinum₹15,000+2×

Tier names, thresholds, and multipliers are fully merchant-configurable. The evaluation window and criterion (spend vs. order count) are program-level settings. Customers auto-promote and auto-demote as their spend crosses thresholds.

India context Indian shoppers expect tiers — inspired by Tata Neu, Flipkart Plus, Zomato Gold. Breeze brings the mechanic to D2C brands at SMB pricing.

Milestones

One-time bonuses awarded when a customer crosses a lifetime threshold:

  • 3rd order → ₹50 bonus
  • ₹5,000 lifetime spend → ₹150 bonus
  • 10th order → ₹500 bonus

Checked automatically after every purchase. Each milestone fires once per customer.

Streaks

Reward consistent purchasing in a rolling window:

  • "3 orders in 30 days → ₹75"
  • "5 orders in 60 days → ₹150"

Each streak achievement is one-per-customer-per-window. When the window resets, the customer can earn it again. Streaks reward consistency rather than spend size — useful for high-frequency low-AOV categories (snacks, beauty, beverages).

Birthday bonus

Automatic credit on the customer's birthday, once per year. Configured globally (e.g. ₹100). Fires at a scheduled job, sends a WhatsApp ("Happy birthday Priya! ₹100 added to your wallet"), idempotent so re-runs don't double-credit.

Profile completion

One-time credit when the customer fills in name, email, and birthday. Default ₹30. Gives merchants better customer data; gives customers a clear "free money" incentive. Tracked field-by-field, fires only when all three are populated.

Newsletter signup

Idempotent reward for first newsletter subscription. Different from the profile completion flow — fires the first time a customer opts into marketing communications.

Spin-the-wheel

Gamified post-purchase reward. Merchant configures a wheel with weighted segments and a daily spin limit. Customer spins after every order (up to the limit), winning a random reward into their wallet. Per-customer daily-limit prevents abuse; weighted probability per segment ensures the merchant's economics work out.

Multiplier stacking

When a customer is in a tier, possibly also a member, and a campaign is running, multipliers compound:

  • Tier multiplier — from the customer's current VIP tier
  • Membership multiplier — from a paid membership (if any). Breeze takes the max of tier and membership, not the product, to avoid runaway boosts.
  • Campaign multiplier — from any active campaign matching the rule. Applied on top of the tier/membership max.

Effective rate is shown on the customer-facing storefront before they buy: "You'll earn 4.5× rewards on this order — your Gold status + the Diwali bonus."

IV.Gift Cards

Pillar 3
Digital gift cards customers can send and redeem.

Gift cards open a category Shopify's native feature doesn't cover well: shareable, claimable, partial-redemption-capable digital gift cards that integrate with the rest of the wallet.

Digital gift card issuance

Merchants issue gift cards from the admin or via the API. Each card has a unique bearer code (alphanumeric, 12–16 characters), an amount, an optional expiry, and an optional recipient name/message. The card is delivered via email or WhatsApp.

Bearer instrument model

A gift card isn't tied to a customer until claimed. Priya can buy a ₹500 card and send the code to her friend; the friend pastes the code into a public claim page (no account required to view), then claims it into their own wallet. The balance moves from the card to the recipient's GiftCard bucket atomically — the same code can't be claimed twice.

Bulk issuance for festive season

Merchants upload a CSV during Diwali / Rakhi / corporate gifting — 500 gift cards generated with unique codes, ready to distribute. Re-upload the same file? Idempotency keys prevent duplicates. Each card has its own ledger trail.

Partial redemption

₹500 card. Customer spends ₹200 at checkout. ₹300 remains in their wallet. They come back for it. Most gift card systems force full-or-nothing; partial creates a strong return motive.

Public balance check

The storefront has a dedicated /s/{slug}/gift-cards/check page where anyone can paste a gift card code and see the remaining balance + expiry. No account required, no friction, no support ticket.

Why it matters Digital gift cards are growing 20–30% annually in India — personal and corporate gifting. Brands without a proper gift card system miss the entire gifting acquisition channel.

V.Referrals

Pillar 4
Two-sided referrals that turn customers into a growth channel.

Word of mouth is the #1 discovery channel for Indian consumers. Breeze makes it systematic.

Auto-generated referral codes

Every customer gets a referral code automatically. Trigger is configurable per merchant — on registration or on first purchase. Codes derive from the customer's name when available (e.g., PRIYA10) or are randomly generated (REF8F3A2B).

Vanity codes

Codes customers actually want to share. PRIYA10. VIKRAM20. KARTHIK. Not ref_8f3a2b_2026. The system reserves customer-name-derived codes by default and falls back to random if collisions occur.

Creator / influencer mode

For brand ambassadors and content creators, the system supports:

  • Custom vanity codes (the creator picks)
  • Tiered commissions per creator
  • Conversion tracking — which creator drove which sale
  • Higher rewards for referee compared to standard customer referrals
  • Public landing pages branded to the creator

A dedicated /admin/influencers page lets the team manage creator codes independently from regular customer referrals.

Anti-fraud signals

Referral programs attract abuse. Breeze tracks five fraud signals on every conversion:

  • Same IP as the referrer? Flag.
  • Same device fingerprint? Flag.
  • Velocity unusual (10+ referrals in an hour)? Flag.
  • Self-referral attempt? Blocked.
  • Referee has a wallet at the merchant already? Flag.

Flagged conversions go to a review queue; the merchant decides whether to honor. Hard-blocked attempts never get logged as conversions.

Branded referral landing pages

Friends land on yourstore.com/refer/PRIYA10 — a full branded page, not a query parameter on the homepage. "Hi! You've been invited by Priya. Get ₹25 off your first order with code PRIYA10." Customer can screenshot it and share with conviction. Conversion uplift versus a query-param link is 2–3×.

Give-get rewards

Both sides get rewarded when the referee completes their first purchase. Referrer's reward goes to their ReferralReward bucket; referee's reward applies at the next checkout. Amounts are configurable per program.

No platform commission

Unlike referral-only tools (ReferralCandy, Friendbuy) that take 1.5–3.5% of referral revenue, Breeze doesn't take a cut. The rewards are entirely yours to design.

VI.Campaigns

Pillar 5
Time-boxed boosts for the festive season and big moments.

Campaigns layer a multiplier on top of one or more existing earn rules for a defined time window. They are the cleanest way to run "double rewards weekend" or "3× cashback during Diwali" without rewriting any rule.

Multiplier campaigns

A campaign specifies:

  • Base rule — which earn rule(s) it applies to
  • Multiplier — 1.5×, 2×, 3×, or any value
  • Start and end dates — scheduled, can run weeks ahead
  • Optional cap override — campaign-specific max if you want to lift caps during the window

During the window, any matching earn event sees its reward scaled by the multiplier. Outside the window, normal rules apply.

Festive templates

Pre-built campaign templates for Indian festivals:

Diwali Rakhi Holi Eid Onam Pongal Independence Day Republic Day New Year Valentine's Day Mother's Day Father's Day

Each template comes with sensible defaults — duration, multiplier, suggested copy. Merchants tweak and ship in under 5 minutes.

Campaign calendar

A unified view of all scheduled campaigns by month — past, active, upcoming. Avoid accidentally stacking three multipliers on one weekend.

Performance tracking

Per-campaign metrics: total orders attributed, unique customers participating, extra rewards issued, marginal revenue lift. Tells the merchant whether the campaign earned its cost.

Stacking with tiers and memberships

Campaign multipliers stack on top of tier and membership multipliers. A Gold customer (1.5×) who is also a member (2×) earns at 2× (max of tier and membership). On Diwali, a 3× campaign multiplies that — effective rate 6×. The customer-facing storefront shows the resolved rate before checkout.

VII.Memberships

Pillar 6
The Amazon Prime model. For your store.

Paid memberships give your best customers VIP treatment — and give you predictable, recurring revenue.

Paid tier model

Merchants offer one or more membership plans — typically monthly and annual. Each plan carries premium benefits:

  • Boosted earn-rate (e.g., 2× cashback on every order)
  • Free shipping
  • Early access to launches
  • Exclusive collections
  • Priority support

Earn-boost stacking

The member's earn-rate multiplier applies on top of their tier. If they're Gold (1.5×) AND a member with a 2× boost, they earn at 2× (max wins). Layer in a Diwali campaign at 3× and the effective rate is 6×. The customer sees this on the storefront: "You earn 6× rewards on this order."

Self-serve upgrade, extend, renew

Members manage their plan from their account. They can:

  • Upgrade from monthly to annual mid-cycle
  • Extend by a custom number of months
  • Renew early (before expiry)
  • Cancel future renewals

No support tickets for plan changes. The wallet, balance, and benefits update instantly when the plan does.

Auto-renewal reminders

WhatsApp nudges fire at 7 days, 1 day, and on expiry. Members renewing close to expiry convert at 65%+ — higher than any other lifecycle moment.

Tied to your loyalty tiers

Members can be granted instant tier status — pay for Gold instead of grinding to it. The customer's effective multiplier is always the better of earned tier and membership tier. No worse off, often better.

Why it matters Members have already committed money to your brand. They will come back to get their money's worth. Typical member spends 3–4× more than non-members.

IX.Merchant operations

Surrounding platform
Built for the people who run loyalty, not just the customers.

Loyalty fails when the merchant team can't operate it. Every screen, every button, every export is built for the human running the store.

Wallet policies

Per credit type (bucket), the merchant configures redemption rules:

  • Minimum redemption — must redeem at least ₹X per transaction
  • Step size — must redeem in multiples of ₹X
  • Max per order (%) — can't pay more than X% of an order from this bucket
  • Max per order (₹) — fixed rupee cap per order
  • Stackable with discount codes? — yes/no toggle
  • Default expiry days — if a rule doesn't specify, use this
  • Excluded payment methods — can't use this bucket on COD orders, for example

The CFO sets the guardrails once; customers see clean rules at checkout.

Manual & bulk credit

Operational tools for the team:

  • Manual credit — credit a single customer for a service recovery, with a reason field for audit.
  • Bulk credit — upload a CSV of customer IDs and an amount; credit 500 customers in one go (e.g. apology for a Diwali shipping delay).

Every entry is logged, reasoned, and attributed to the team member who issued it.

Debit & force-expire

Mistakes happen. Tools for cleanup:

  • Admin debit — claw back a wrongly-issued credit. Reason required.
  • Force expire — expire a stale balance ahead of schedule.

Every reversal lands in the same append-only ledger — auditable, never silent.

The actual WhatsApp / SMS / email copy is yours to edit per event. Tone, language, emoji. Hindi, Tamil, Gujarati — go. Customer detail page

X.Built on — trust & audit

Surrounding platform
Built like a bank. Priced like a SaaS.

Loyalty programs are real liability — points are a number on your balance sheet (under Ind AS 115 / IFRS 15). The system that mints them had better be one you'd trust your auditor with.

Append-only double-entry ledger

Every credit, debit, COD release, and expiry is an immutable entry. Nothing is overwritten. Nothing is silently corrected. The ledger is the source of truth — your auditor will recognize the shape immediately.

Idempotency on every action

Re-process the same Shopify webhook 100 times. The customer earns the bonus once. Every operation that touches the wallet carries an idempotency key — duplicates are physically impossible.

Event ingestion state machine

Shopify webhooks land in a state machine: Received → Processing → Processed (or Failed). Nothing is silently dropped, nothing is silently retried. Operations sees the truth on a dashboard, not in customer complaints.

Full attribution on every change

Every credit attributes to a rule, a campaign, a manual issuance, or a system event — and the human who triggered it. Customer asks "where did this ₹150 come from?" — one query, one answer.

The eight foundational truths

The architectural commitments we never break:

  1. The ledger is append-only — entries are immutable; corrections are new entries.
  2. Idempotency is structural — every operation has a stable key; duplicate keys are blocked at the storage layer.
  3. Value is not fungible across buckets — earned credit ≠ gift card balance ≠ refund credit; conversion rules are explicit.
  4. Held credit is held — pending COD credit is a real ledger state, not a placeholder.
  5. Movements have direction — every entry is In, Out, or Held; balance is the algebraic sum.
  6. Identity is by phone first — Indian customers identify primarily by phone; email is secondary.
  7. Rules are snapshotted at evaluation — a rule edit doesn't retroactively change historical earnings.
  8. Events fire from real triggers — every reward has an event source we can point at.

These weren't bolted on as we built. They were the architectural decisions before the first feature shipped.

XI.India-first by design

Surrounding platform
Not adapted to India. Built for India.

COD-aware mechanics

The held-credit model covered in Part II isn't an afterthought — it's the platform's signature feature. COD is 60%+ of Indian D2C volume; treating it as a second-class case (the way every global loyalty platform does) means giving up most of the program's reach.

WhatsApp as the primary channel

Global tools default to email. India defaults to WhatsApp. Open rates: 85% vs 18%. Breeze treats WhatsApp as the primary notification channel; email is a secondary fallback. The connector framework supports multiple Indian WhatsApp BSPs (Interakt, WATI) — swap providers without changing templates or losing history.

Festive-native campaigns

Diwali, Rakhi, Holi, Eid, Onam, Pongal — every major Indian festival has a campaign template with sensible defaults. Brands ship festive promotions in minutes, not days. Global tools treat festivals as "create a custom campaign yourself, good luck."

UPI integration

Customers can fund their wallet via UPI — Breeze accepts deposits through standard UPI flows and credits the customer's CustomerFunded bucket. Useful for prepaid program design and pre-festive sale runs ("Add ₹500, get ₹525 credit").

RTO analytics

COD-aware design needs RTO awareness. Breeze tracks per-merchant RTO rates and shows the financial impact: "Your COD rate is 65%; your COD-to-prepaid conversion is 12%; that saved ₹2.4L in logistics last month."

Multi-lingual notification templates

Notification templates support Hindi, Tamil, Gujarati, Bengali, Marathi, and English out of the box. Brands targeting Tier-2/Tier-3 cities pick the language per template; can also segment by customer language preference.

INR-first pricing & accounting

The wallet's currency is INR. Pricing is in INR. Bookkeeping integrations target Tally and Zoho. No conversion confusion, no FX baked into the customer's view.

XIII.Getting started

Context

Onboarding flow

The setup wizard walks merchants through six steps:

  1. Welcome — confirm the merchant and Shopify connection.
  2. Rewards — set the prepaid cashback rate (%), minimum order amount, max cashback per order. Optionally enable COD rewards at a different rate.
  3. Wallet policy — minimum redemption, max % of order, whether credits stack with discount codes.
  4. Loyalty tiers — opt into a default Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum ladder, or skip.
  5. Referrals — opt into a two-sided referral program with default rewards, or skip.
  6. Done — summary of everything created, links to the dashboard and the storefront.

The wizard's defaults are conservative and sensible for a typical Indian D2C brand. Everything is editable post-onboarding.

Common merchant questions

Q: Why didn't my customer get points on their order?

Check, in order: (1) Is the rule active? (2) Do the order conditions match (payment method, min amount, etc.)? (3) Is it a COD order? COD rewards are held, not denied — they release after delivery webhook fires. (4) Did the event ingestion fail? Check the Events log under Transactions.

Q: Customer paid via UPI but earned the COD cashback rate.

Shopify's payment_method field. If it's coming through as "cash on delivery," it'll match the COD rule. Check the order's payment_gateway_names in the event payload. If Shopify is mis-tagging, the merchant needs to fix it in Shopify checkout settings.

Q: How do I refund a customer's cashback if they returned the order?

For COD orders: the logistics RTO webhook automatically voids the held credit. No action needed. For prepaid orders: use the Customer detail page → "Debit" action. Reason field required for audit.

Q: Can I run multiple campaigns at the same time?

Yes. If two campaigns match the same rule, multipliers don't compound — the higher multiplier wins. If they target different rules, both apply.

Q: What happens when a tier's threshold is changed?

Customers are re-evaluated at the next reward event. A customer who was Gold under the old threshold may drop to Silver if their spend no longer qualifies. The change isn't retroactive — past earnings are locked in.

Q: How is liability tracked?

The transactions ledger gives the running total of outstanding credit per bucket. Total liability = sum across buckets minus any pending COD (which is contingent until delivery).

Quick-reference operational tips

  • Always check the Events log before assuming a bug. Most "missing credit" issues are event ingestion problems (Shopify webhook didn't fire, event was rejected, etc.).
  • For high-priority support cases, the Customer detail page + the audit trail on each ledger entry is your fastest path to root cause.
  • When onboarding a new merchant, run them through the preview wizard so they understand what's set up — then point them at /admin/loyalty to tune tier thresholds based on their actual customer data.
  • For festive launches: set up campaigns 1–2 weeks ahead so notifications can fire announcement messages before the campaign starts.
  • Use bulk-credit (NOT manual credit per customer) when crediting more than 5 customers at once. Better audit trail.

Appendix A — Glossary

Bucket
A category of credit within a wallet. Each bucket has its own rules. Examples: EarnedCredit, CodPending, GiftCard.
Cap (max_amount)
The maximum reward a single rule will pay per order. Set on each earn rule.
Cap mode
How a rule's cap interacts with multipliers. Hard (cap is absolute), Soft (multipliers exceed cap), or Custom per-tier.
Connector
A merchant-configured integration with an external service (WhatsApp BSP, logistics provider, etc.).
COD pending
Credit held after a COD order is placed, awaiting delivery confirmation.
Earn rule
A configuration that defines when and how much a customer earns. Has conditions, calculation, and action.
Effective multiplier
The combined multiplier applied to a reward, computed from tier × campaign × max(loyalty, membership).
Held entry
A ledger entry that exists but isn't yet spendable. Used for COD pending credits.
Idempotency key
A stable identifier on every operation that prevents duplicate processing.
Ledger entry
An immutable record of a wallet movement. Append-only.
Multiplier
A factor applied to a base reward. Comes from VIP tier, membership, or campaign.
RTO
Return to Origin. A COD order that was placed but not accepted at delivery.
Rule snapshot
A frozen copy of a rule at the moment it fired, so subsequent rule edits don't change historical reward attribution.
Tier
A status level a customer achieves through spend or order count. Carries an earn-rate multiplier.
VIP tier multiplier
The earn-rate multiplier tied to a tier. Bronze 1×, Silver 1.25×, etc.
Wallet policy
Per-bucket redemption rules: minimum redemption, max per order, expiry, stackability.

The eight foundational truths (recap)

  1. The ledger is append-only.
  2. Idempotency is structural.
  3. Value is not fungible across buckets.
  4. Held credit is held.
  5. Movements have direction.
  6. Identity is by phone first.
  7. Rules are snapshotted at evaluation.
  8. Events fire from real triggers.
