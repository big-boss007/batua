What was fixed

The .modal-body container inside CampaignDetailModal.svelte was not stretching to fill the modal's slot content area. The Modal component's .slot-content uses display: flex, but the child .modal-body didn't have width: 100%, so it only took its intrinsic content width.

Fix: Added width: 100% to .modal-body in frontend/src/lib/client/modules/rules/ui/CampaignDetailModal.svelte.
