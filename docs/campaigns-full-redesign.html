Campaign Settings
⚙️ Multiplier Stacking Mode

How campaign multipliers combine with tier/membership multipliers when a customer earns rewards

Multiplicative (Recommended)
Campaign and tier multipliers multiply together. Gold members benefit more from campaigns.
Gold 2x × Campaign 2x = 4x total
Best-of
Customer gets the higher of campaign or tier multiplier, not both.
Gold 2x vs Campaign 3x = 3x total (campaign wins)
Additive
Campaign bonus is added to tier multiplier. More predictable costs.
Gold 2x + Campaign 2x = 3x total (1 + 1 + 1)
