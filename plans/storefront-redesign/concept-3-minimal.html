Key Design Changes
1
Hero Balance: The balance is THE centerpiece — massive 56px font, center-aligned, with nothing competing for attention. Pending amount called out in amber. No gradient card, no chrome.
2
Stats Row: Bucket breakdown as a three-column stat row with subtle dividers. Adds a third "On Hold" metric. Clean, scannable, no progress bars needed.
3
Segmented Tier Progress: Instead of a single bar, progress to Silver is shown as 6 discrete segments. Feels like filling up a meter in a game — each segment is a mini-milestone.
4
Date-Grouped Transactions: Transactions grouped by date (Yesterday, March 17, March 15) instead of a flat list. Gives temporal context and makes it easy to correlate with purchases.
5
Color-Bar Indicators: Each transaction has a thin vertical color bar (green=credit, red=debit, purple=transfer) instead of icon circles. Subtler, more elegant, and takes less space.
6
Horizontal Dividers as Architecture: Thin dividers separate content zones instead of cards. Removes visual clutter of card borders/shadows while maintaining clear section hierarchy.
