Key Design Changes
1
XP-based Progression: Reframes "points" as "XP" with a gaming-style level card. The rainbow gradient top border and shield icon create excitement around tier progression.
2
Streak Tracking: A "🔥 3-day streak" badge in the header gamifies return visits. Creates a habit loop — users check in to maintain their streak.
3
Achievement Badges: Horizontal scrolling achievement row shows unlocked milestones (First Purchase, ₹100 Saved, Repeat Buyer) and teases locked ones. Drives engagement through collectible mechanics.
4
XP Earned per Transaction: Each transaction shows XP earned alongside the amount. Reinforces that every purchase is progress toward the next tier.
5
Personalized Greeting: "Hey there! 👋" with contextual subtitle creates warmth. Could be personalized with name if available ("Hey Chirag!").
6
Separated Balance & Tier: Balance is its own card (not competing with tier visually). Bucket breakdown uses colored dot pills — simpler and more scannable than progress bars.
