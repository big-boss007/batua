What's New in 3B
1
Customer Name in Header: "Priya Sharma" sits below the brand name in the header — always visible, no extra vertical space. Compact and contextual.
2
CTA-Framed Balance: "Ready to spend" eyebrow + "Use on your next order" button reframes the balance as an action, not just a number. Drives conversion back to the store.
3
Expiry Warning: Amber banner surfaces time-sensitive info — "₹45 expires in 12 days." Creates urgency without being pushy. Only shown when points are near expiration.
4
Ways to Earn Section: Three earn pathways (Shop, Refer, Review) with clear reward amounts. Educates customers on the full program and drives engagement beyond just checking balance.
5
Information Hierarchy: What you can spend → what's urgent → how to earn more → your progress → what happened. Forward-looking, not backward-looking.
