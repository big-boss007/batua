Design Rules
1
Active tier badge shows the effective tier — whichever is higher between earned and membership. If membership is active and higher, the badge shows the membership tier name.
2
"Member" tag + days remaining appears only when the displayed tier comes from a membership. Absent when showing a purely earned tier.
3
Section title changes — "Your Tier" when membership is active (it's an assigned tier), "Loyalty Tier" when showing purely earned (it's organic).
4
Earn progress always shows the organic loyalty progression — "Earning toward Silver" (the next tier the customer can reach by accumulating points). This never changes based on membership. Label says "Earning toward X" to distinguish from the displayed tier.
5
Separated by a divider — the active tier badge and the earn progress bar are visually separated. The top is "what you have now", the bottom is "what you're working toward".
