Key Design Changes
1
Glassmorphism Balance Card: Frosted-glass effect with subtle radial glow replaces the flat gradient. Feels premium and modern, like a credit card floating in space.
2
Chip-style Bucket Breakdown: Instead of inline progress bars, buckets are shown as distinct chips with their own mini progress bars. Cleaner separation, easier to scan.
3
Circular Tier Progress Ring: Replaces the linear progress bar with a ring chart — more visually engaging and immediately communicates completion percentage at a glance.
4
Quick Action Grid: Promotes Gift Card, Balance Details, and Referral into always-visible icon buttons instead of burying them as text links at the bottom.
5
Human-readable Labels: "EarnedCredit" → "Earned", "CodPending" → "COD Pending". Raw bucket type names are replaced with friendly labels throughout.
6
Compact Header: Phone number shown as a masked badge in the header bar, freeing up vertical space previously used by the "Showing rewards for your account" banner.
